Privacy policy

Last updated 20 October 2026. This timetable runs in your browser and does not require an account.

Cookies. If you switch themes, a gmi-theme cookie remembers that choice for up to one year. Switching between the grid, week and agenda views stores a gmi-view cookie the same way, and hiding or showing the class panel stores a gmi-rail cookie. None of these cookies are used for analytics, advertising or tracking.

On-device storage. Your selected class, seen timetable revision and accessibility preferences are saved in this browser’s local storage. They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Timetable files. DaVinci files you open are processed locally in your browser. They are not uploaded and are not kept after you refresh the page. Calendar downloads are generated locally.

External links. Links to GitHub or calendar providers open third-party sites with their own privacy policies. Clear this site’s cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings.

Questions or requests can be raised through the project’s GitHub issue tracker .
